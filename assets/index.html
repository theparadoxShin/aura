AQHI+ is the wildfire-smoke override used in Alberta and British Columbia: the ceiling of the hourly PM2.5 mean divided by 10, taking the higher of that and the three-pollutant AQHI. AURA measures PM2.5 but not NO₂ or O₃, so the published value is driven by the smoke term — the case AURA is built for. Readings are local to the device, not a regional station.
